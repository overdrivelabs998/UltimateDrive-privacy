# Privacy Policy — Ultimate Drive: Racing Manager

**Effective date:** 28 July 2026
**Developer:** Overdrive Labs
**App:** Ultimate Drive: Racing Manager (`com.overdrivelabs.ultimatedrive`)
**Contact:** overdrivelabs998@gmail.com

Ultimate Drive: Racing Manager ("the Game", "we", "us") is a single-player racing team manager game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the Game.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do **not** require an account, and we do **not** ourselves collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or contacts).

Game progress (season, budget, upgrades, settings) is saved **only on your device** and is never uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows advertisements served by **Google AdMob**, including:

- **Banner ads** displayed in menus.
- **Rewarded ads** that you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game currency. Rewarded ads are always optional — watching them is never required to play.

To serve and measure ads, Google may automatically collect certain data from your device, such as:

- Advertising ID (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
- IP address and coarse location derived from it
- Device information (model, OS version, language)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks, reward completions)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses ad data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- AdMob data practices: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## 3. Your Choices

- **Personalized ads:** You can opt out of ads personalization on Android under *Settings → Google → Ads* ("Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization").
- **Consent:** Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), you will be shown a consent form before personalized ads are served, and you may choose non-personalized ads.
- **Rewarded ads** are shown only when you tap to watch them.

## 4. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving is configured in line with Google Play Families and AdMob policies. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us and we will address it.

## 5. Data Security & Retention

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies linked above.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
